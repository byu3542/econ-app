# UX Audit - 2026-08-10

Audit of the Economic Dashboard app against `UX_DESIGN_LAWS.md` (21 laws). Reviewed by
inspection only (no build available). Code refs are to `app/src/main/`. Note: the codebase is
now fully Kotlin (88 `.kt`, 0 `.java`) following migration batches 0-12c.

## Summary
- **Coverage:** 12 covered · 7 partial · 1 gap · 1 regression risk across 21 laws.
- **Since last audit/tickets:** Kotlin migration landed app-wide; retry-chip logic was
  extracted into `utils/RetryChip.kt` and is wired in all 10 leaf fragments plus Dashboard;
  `ui/views/PressFeedback.kt` and `SegmentedControl.kt` shipped (IOS-01); `SkeletonController`
  extended skeletons to 8 leaf screens; historical Room cache expanded 6→34 series.
- **Top opportunities (start here):**
  1. **Progressive Disclosure (18):** `MetricBottomSheet` is wired on only 4 of 10 leaf
     fragments (Wages, Inflation, Cpi, Gdp) + Dashboard — Stocks, Bonds, Spreads, Employment,
     Housing, Yields don't route taps to the detail sheet.
  2. **Regression risk — Zeigarnik/return-visit:** the "since you last opened" strip
     (`getSinceLastOpen`) no longer exists anywhere in the codebase; it likely fell out
     during Kotlin batch 12a dead-code removal.
  3. **Accessibility (21):** `contentDescription` is essentially absent from data layouts —
     0 occurrences in `fragment_dashboard.xml` and `card_kpi_badge.xml`; charts and icon
     buttons are invisible to TalkBack.
  4. **Doherty (9):** Wages and Cpi are the two leaf screens still without
     `SkeletonController`; Yields lacks `attachCrosshair`; Yields and Treasury lack the
     timeframe selector.
  5. **Tesler (14):** `DerivedMetricsBuilder` output is consumed only by
     `AiAnalystBottomSheet` — derived metrics (real wage growth, spreads, YoY) never surface
     as first-class values on cards.

## Details worth expanding

**Progressive disclosure stopped at 40% of leaf screens (law 18 → TICKET-30).** The
tap-card-for-detail contract is the app's core interaction promise. `MetricBottomSheet.kt`
survived the Kotlin migration, but only `DashboardFragment`, `WagesFragment`,
`InflationFragment`, `CpiFragment`, and `GdpFragment` reference it. On Stocks, Bonds, Spreads,
Employment, Housing, and Yields a tap on a metric goes nowhere, which reads as broken rather
than merely missing.

**The "since you last opened" strip vanished (law 11 → TICKET-31).** History markers list it
as shipped (P4 era, `getSinceLastOpen()` in the ViewModel), but no symbol matching
`sinceLastOpen`/`lastOpen` exists anywhere in `app/src/main/java` today. Kotlin batch 12a was
explicitly a dead-code-removal pass — the most likely culprit. Either it was intentionally cut
(then the docs should say so) or it was collateral damage and should be restored.

**TalkBack coverage is the one true gap (law 21 → TICKET-32).** `DeltaFormatter.kt` does the
hard part — direction is triple-encoded (glyph, sign, colorblind-aware color). But the layouts
never describe anything: zero `contentDescription` in the dashboard layout and the workhorse
KPI card. Charts, sparklines, and icon-only buttons are silent to screen readers.

**Skeleton/crosshair/timeframe parity is three screens from done (laws 1, 9 → TICKET-33/34).**
`SkeletonController` covers 8 of 10 leaves — Wages and Cpi still lack it. `attachCrosshair` is
on 9 of 10 — Yields lacks it. The timeframe selector include is absent from
`fragment_yields.xml` and `fragment_treasury.xml`. Small, mechanical finishes.

**Derived metrics are AI-only (law 14 → TICKET-35).** `DerivedMetricsBuilder.kt` computes
real wage growth, spreads, and YoY deltas, but its only consumer is `AiAnalystBottomSheet.kt`.
The user shouldn't need to ask the AI for a number the app already computed.
